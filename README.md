# Bibby Brickwork Training

Construction Dust and Respiratory Protection Training Course for Bibby Brickwork Limited: one link sent out on WhatsApp,
12 modules, assessment, declaration, and the evidence records behind them (register, individual training records, HSE evidence pack).
Built and supported by Safety Simplified Ltd.

Hosted on Railway (Node.js, PostgreSQL). Variables: `DATABASE_URL`, `MASTER_EMAIL`, `MASTER_PASSWORD`, `NODE_ENV=production`, optional `PUBLIC_URL`, `SESSION_SECRET`.

The course text, question bank and answer key are in `client/course.js`. Changing them means a new revision number.
